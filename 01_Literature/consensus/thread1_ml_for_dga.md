# Brief on **DGA Fault-Diagnosis** Literature

This brief covers **classifier performance, review-level limitations, and comparison with conventional DGA**, plus explainability and class-imbalance handling. The strongest recent evidence favors ensemble models built on engineered gas-ratio features, but many studies still use small, mixed-provenance datasets and weak validation designs [[1.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/identification-and-application-of-machine-learning-rao-fofana/7bea68d78e0a5cb28a98e4106594a36d/) [[1.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/identification-and-application-of-machine-learning-rao-fofana/7bea68d78e0a5cb28a98e4106594a36d/) [[1.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/identification-and-application-of-machine-learning-rao-fofana/7bea68d78e0a5cb28a98e4106594a36d/) [[2.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/multiclass-fault-diagnosis-in-power-transformers-using-adekunle-fofana/ebfe271711c553849b8c64b95d76c852/) [[3.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/dissolved-gas-analysis-for-fault-prediction-in-power-al-sakini-bilal/d270879525e95ff0a548bfcad4d3dbbb/) [[4.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-diagnosis-of-transformer-using-artificial-zhang-tang/f3e53e58b8a15f2d8195690181752c41/).

## Classifier Studies

The classifier literature is dominated by SVM, ANN, KNN, RF, and newer boosting ensembles, usually trained on raw gas concentrations, gas ratios, or conventional DGA outputs converted into ML features [[2.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/multiclass-fault-diagnosis-in-power-transformers-using-adekunle-fofana/ebfe271711c553849b8c64b95d76c852/) [[4.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-diagnosis-of-transformer-using-artificial-zhang-tang/f3e53e58b8a15f2d8195690181752c41/) [[2.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/multiclass-fault-diagnosis-in-power-transformers-using-adekunle-fofana/ebfe271711c553849b8c64b95d76c852/) [[3.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/dissolved-gas-analysis-for-fault-prediction-in-power-al-sakini-bilal/d270879525e95ff0a548bfcad4d3dbbb/). ## Reviews And Limitations

Review papers and broad introductions converge on the same weaknesses: **limited data availability**, class imbalance, overlapping gas signatures, and the difficulty of obtaining publishable utility datasets because of confidentiality [[1.6]](https://consensus.app/papers/identification-and-application-of-machine-learning-rao-fofana/7bea68d78e0a5cb28a98e4106594a36d/) [[8.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/machine-learning-based-multimethod-interpretation-to-suwarno-sutikno/d0d2866090bd5b7bb6e323741670ced7/) [[8.6]](https://consensus.app/papers/machine-learning-based-multimethod-interpretation-to-suwarno-sutikno/d0d2866090bd5b7bb6e323741670ced7/) [[5.6]](https://consensus.app/papers/accurate-identification-of-transformer-faults-from-das-paramane/e7b84c30e6e753b0af83509ad16c0a4d/) [[3.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/dissolved-gas-analysis-for-fault-prediction-in-power-al-sakini-bilal/d270879525e95ff0a548bfcad4d3dbbb/). Reviews also stress unresolved-code problems, boundary sensitivity in ratio rules, and the need for feature selection, temporal information, and better imbalance treatment [[4.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-diagnosis-of-transformer-using-artificial-zhang-tang/f3e53e58b8a15f2d8195690181752c41/) [[4.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-diagnosis-of-transformer-using-artificial-zhang-tang/f3e53e58b8a15f2d8195690181752c41/) [[4.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-diagnosis-of-transformer-using-artificial-zhang-tang/f3e53e58b8a15f2d8195690181752c41/).

- **Small, imbalanced datasets** remain a recurring barrier to robust deployment [[3.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/dissolved-gas-analysis-for-fault-prediction-in-power-al-sakini-bilal/d270879525e95ff0a548bfcad4d3dbbb/) [[5.6]](https://consensus.app/papers/accurate-identification-of-transformer-faults-from-das-paramane/e7b84c30e6e753b0af83509ad16c0a4d/) [[16.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-diagnosis-in-power-transformers-using-resampled-thavasilingam-sivaraju/f20752a92ca2537cabf4dd234bb45af4/).
- **Mixed-provenance labels** are common, with several studies combining utility, literature, and rule-based labels rather than fully independent ground truth [[3.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/dissolved-gas-analysis-for-fault-prediction-in-power-al-sakini-bilal/d270879525e95ff0a548bfcad4d3dbbb/) [[10.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-classification-in-power-transformers-using-dladla-thango/4a69819fb5595b0caec6b9046406778b/) [[17.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/gaussian-process-multiclass-classification-for-wang-littler/9b01047429395160af0a98802603d76e/).
- **Reproducibility and deployment** are still weakly evidenced, because many studies report high internal accuracy without independent external validation [[1.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/identification-and-application-of-machine-learning-rao-fofana/7bea68d78e0a5cb28a98e4106594a36d/) [[5.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/accurate-identification-of-transformer-faults-from-das-paramane/e7b84c30e6e753b0af83509ad16c0a4d/) [[2.6]](https://consensus.app/papers/multiclass-fault-diagnosis-in-power-transformers-using-adekunle-fofana/ebfe271711c553849b8c64b95d76c852/).

## Conventional Versus ML

Papers directly comparing ML with conventional DGA methods on the same samples usually find higher accuracy and fewer ambiguous decisions for ML or hybrid systems [[3.7]](https://consensus.app/papers/dissolved-gas-analysis-for-fault-prediction-in-power-al-sakini-bilal/d270879525e95ff0a548bfcad4d3dbbb/) [[8.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/machine-learning-based-multimethod-interpretation-to-suwarno-sutikno/d0d2866090bd5b7bb6e323741670ced7/) [[10.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-classification-in-power-transformers-using-dladla-thango/4a69819fb5595b0caec6b9046406778b/) [[17.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/gaussian-process-multiclass-classification-for-wang-littler/9b01047429395160af0a98802603d76e/). Conventional methods are criticized for inconsistent conclusions on the same oil sample and for out-of-code or no-diagnosis cases when gas ratios lie outside rule boundaries [[8.7]](https://consensus.app/papers/machine-learning-based-multimethod-interpretation-to-suwarno-sutikno/d0d2866090bd5b7bb6e323741670ced7/) [[8.8]](https://consensus.app/papers/machine-learning-based-multimethod-interpretation-to-suwarno-sutikno/d0d2866090bd5b7bb6e323741670ced7/) [[14.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/a-unified-and-interpretable-benchmark-of-classification-doğan-ertarğın/b6a9e032e22b5c5b8ff835d89564e8c3/) [[17.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/gaussian-process-multiclass-classification-for-wang-littler/9b01047429395160af0a98802603d76e/).

Studies using the same datasets for both approaches report large gaps. Optimized ML in *Machines* 2025 reached **92.478%** versus **35.757%** for the Duval Triangle and **30.818%** for the Duval Pentagon [[10.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-classification-in-power-transformers-using-dladla-thango/4a69819fb5595b0caec6b9046406778b/) [[10.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-classification-in-power-transformers-using-dladla-thango/4a69819fb5595b0caec6b9046406778b/). Tree-ensemble models validated by Hechifa et al. exceeded the best traditional comparators, with validation accuracies above **89.89–92.13%** versus **83.15%** for clustering, **82.02%** for the combined technique, **80.90%** for modified IEC 60599, and **79.78%** for key-gas-with-ratio methods [[7.6]](https://consensus.app/papers/improved-intelligent-methods-for-power-transformer-fault-hechifa-lakehal/b2893c8403255eba8aeb4be640780165/) [[7.7]](https://consensus.app/papers/improved-intelligent-methods-for-power-transformer-fault-hechifa-lakehal/b2893c8403255eba8aeb4be640780165/). The core problem conventional methods face is visible in papers emphasizing disagreement among Rogers, IEC, Doernenburg, key-gas, and Duval outputs for the same sample. **Figure 2:** Accuracy comparison of integrated and conventional DGA methods [[8]](https://consensus.app/papers/machine-learning-based-multimethod-interpretation-to-suwarno-sutikno/d0d2866090bd5b7bb6e323741670ced7/)


## Explainability And Imbalance

Explainability in this area is still emerging, but the recent evidence is coherent. SHAP-based studies found that engineered relative concentration features and ratios such as **CO/H2** were among the most influential predictors, and CatBoost feature-importance analysis likewise highlighted hydrogen and engineered ratios [[14.6]](https://consensus.app/papers/a-unified-and-interpretable-benchmark-of-classification-doğan-ertarğın/b6a9e032e22b5c5b8ff835d89564e8c3/) [[12.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/a-catboostbased-framework-for-datadriven-fault-diagnosis-tanjim-ahmed/be7cbb190f945761849fbcc0ae7f3627/) [[12.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/a-catboostbased-framework-for-datadriven-fault-diagnosis-tanjim-ahmed/be7cbb190f945761849fbcc0ae7f3627/). XGBoost with centroid-distance features derived from Duval pentagon, Duval triangle, and IEC coordinates reached **92.8%** mean accuracy and then used SHAP to explain indicator contributions [[18.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-diagnosis-of-power-transformer-based-on-centroid-li-liao/4a0209a2e50a521eb932b3bd637f913a/) [[18.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-diagnosis-of-power-transformer-based-on-centroid-li-liao/4a0209a2e50a521eb932b3bd637f913a/) [[18.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-diagnosis-of-power-transformer-based-on-centroid-li-liao/4a0209a2e50a521eb932b3bd637f913a/) [[18.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-diagnosis-of-power-transformer-based-on-centroid-li-liao/4a0209a2e50a521eb932b3bd637f913a/).

- **Engineered ratios** are common inputs, including Rogers, IEC, Duval percentages, and new hybrid ratios [[3.8]](https://consensus.app/papers/dissolved-gas-analysis-for-fault-prediction-in-power-al-sakini-bilal/d270879525e95ff0a548bfcad4d3dbbb/) [[2.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/multiclass-fault-diagnosis-in-power-transformers-using-adekunle-fofana/ebfe271711c553849b8c64b95d76c852/) [[15.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/power-transformer-fault-diagnosis-based-on-dga-using-a-taha-ibrahim/5bfcfc3e2dda5444b2973a2e365623e8/).
- **SMOTE** is the most explicit imbalance remedy in the supplied studies, improving RF- and ratio-based classifiers [[5.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/accurate-identification-of-transformer-faults-from-das-paramane/e7b84c30e6e753b0af83509ad16c0a4d/) [[6.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/precise-transformer-fault-diagnosis-via-random-forest-prasojo-putra/f627a42f71db585380dcdd903700d52b/) [[6.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/precise-transformer-fault-diagnosis-via-random-forest-prasojo-putra/f627a42f71db585380dcdd903700d52b/).
- **More advanced resampling** also appears, with SMOTE-ENN and ADASYN supporting up to **98.7%** recognition for optimized nonlinear SVM in a 2026 study [[16.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-diagnosis-in-power-transformers-using-resampled-thavasilingam-sivaraju/f20752a92ca2537cabf4dd234bb45af4/) [[16.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-diagnosis-in-power-transformers-using-resampled-thavasilingam-sivaraju/f20752a92ca2537cabf4dd234bb45af4/) [[16.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/fault-diagnosis-in-power-transformers-using-resampled-thavasilingam-sivaraju/f20752a92ca2537cabf4dd234bb45af4/).

For an MPhil chapter, the clearest synthesis is that **ensemble models with ratio-engineered features** now lead DGA fault classification, conventional methods remain useful as feature generators but are weaker as stand-alone classifiers, and the main unresolved issues are dataset quality, independent validation, and reproducible deployment. Across the supplied literature, explainability and imbalance handling are growing but are still far less mature than raw accuracy reporting.
