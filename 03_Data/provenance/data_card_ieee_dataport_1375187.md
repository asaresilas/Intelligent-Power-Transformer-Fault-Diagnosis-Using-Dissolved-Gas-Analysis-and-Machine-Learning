- **Step 5 findings (2026-10-10, `03_Data/interim/step05_report.md`):** hashes of all three files re-verified. (1) The value **0.0001 ppm is used as a placeholder**: 257 cells in DGA_train, 34 in DGA_test_unseen, none in IEC_TC_10_data. It is far below any laboratory detection limit, so it is not a measurement; it stands for "not available / not detected". True zeros also occur (train 100, test 5, IEC file 30). (2) **DGA_train contains IEC TC 10 cases, some duplicated**: e.g. lines 111 and 137 are both the IEC TC 10 D2 case "Short circuit from LV to ground, internal" (H2 7020, CH4 1850, C2H4 2960, C2H2 4410; the paper's dash for C2H6 became 0.0001); lines 368 and 393 are both the D2 case "Arcing in wet cellulose". Leakage risk for the IEC TC 10 benchmark → full overlap audit in Step 9. (3) **IEC_TC_10_data.xlsx is not purely Annex 1 of Duval & dePablo (2001)**: it has 15 Normal records (Annex 1 has none) and non-integer values (e.g. 0.7, 3.3) whereas every Annex 1 value is an integer. Origin of those rows TO VERIFY in Step 9. These findings bear directly on decision D7.
- **Step 9 findings (2026-10-10):** DGA_train contains **96 IEC TC 10 cases** (removed from development for leakage), 125 exact copies of Li 2019 records, and 22 records matching DGA_test_unseen (1 exact, 21 near); 2 exact within-file copies and 4 records with identical gases but different labels were also removed. Development after cleaning: 439 records. Label agreement on copied IEC cases: 132 of 136; the 4 disagreements are IEC T1/T2 cases labelled T3 (3) and PD (1). DGA_test_unseen: 9 records duplicate IEC TC 10 cases (61 independent). IEC_TC_10_data.xlsx: 31 of 49 rows match Annex 1; 18 do not (incl. 15 Normal) — not used in any role. See decision D7 (closed) and D9.
